Accessibility Statement

This website serves older adults and their families — accessibility is the point, not a checkbox. We design toward WCAG 2.1 AA and test on an ongoing basis.

Readable typography Body text of 17–20px, generous line height, and no low-contrast gray text.
Strong color contrast Text and interactive elements are designed to meet WCAG AA contrast ratios against their backgrounds.
Keyboard access Navigation, forms, accordions, and toggles are operable by keyboard, with visible focus outlines throughout.
Labeled forms Every field carries a visible label above it — no placeholder-only inputs — with large touch targets.
Alternative text Meaningful images carry descriptive alt text; decorative graphics are hidden from screen readers.
Responsive layouts Single-column mobile layouts, comparison tables that become stacked cards, and no content that requires horizontal scrolling.
Calm by design No auto-moving carousels, countdown timers, flashing elements, or intrusive popups.

We do not claim complete WCAG conformance — that claim belongs only after formal testing, which has not yet been completed. Known issues are prioritized and fixed as part of ongoing work.

Having difficulty using this website?

Tell us what page you were on and what happened: support@agingathomeadvisor.com, or use the contact form with the “Accessibility issue” subject. Accessibility reports are treated with the same priority as errors.
