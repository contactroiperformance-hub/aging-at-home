ABOUT US

Independent guidance for aging safely at home

Aging at Home Advisor helps older adults and their families understand home accessibility options, compare typical costs, learn about financial assistance, and connect with qualified professionals — without pressure and without jargon.

What we do

Explain the options

Plain-language guides to tub-to-shower conversions, walk-in showers and tubs, and accessible remodeling — what’s involved, what it costs, and what to watch for.

Clarify the money side

Honest cost overviews and a careful guide to financial assistance programs — including what they typically do not cover.

Connect you locally

When you’re ready, share your ZIP code and project details and we’ll help you see whether qualified professionals serve your area. Free, with no obligation.

How we make money

Aging at Home Advisor is free for consumers. When you request information, we may be compensated by home improvement professionals or their networks for connecting you. This compensation does not change our editorial guidance, and it never obligates you to hire anyone.
