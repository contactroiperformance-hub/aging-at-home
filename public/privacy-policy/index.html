6. How information is disclosed

We disclose personal information to these categories of recipients:

  • LeadByte (our CRM, acting as a service provider processing data on our instructions)
  • A call-tracking provider (to be named before call tracking is enabled)
  • Google (analytics and tag management)
  • Meta (advertising measurement)
  • Cloudflare (hosting, DNS, security, delivery)
  • Professional advisors
  • Government or law-enforcement authorities where legally required
  • Successors in a merger, asset transfer, financing, or sale

Independent professionals receiving a lead may become separate businesses responsible for their own use of the information. Their services, privacy practices, contracts, prices, and work are separate from Aging at Home Advisor.

7. Sale, sharing, and targeted advertising

Aging at Home Advisor may disclose project inquiries to home-improvement professionals in connection with its referral and lead-generation business, and may use Meta Pixel and similar advertising technologies. Some US privacy laws may define one or more of these activities as a “sale” of personal information, “sharing” of personal information, or “targeted advertising.”

You may exercise applicable opt-out rights through Your Privacy Choices. We also honor recognized Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals: when your browser sends GPC, advertising technologies do not load and the advertising opt-out is saved automatically.

One distinction matters: advertising tracking (Meta Pixel, analytics) is passive measurement you can opt out of at any time. A contractor referral is different — it happens only when you actively submit the lead form asking us to share your project inquiry. Opting out of advertising never blocks the lead form, and cookie preferences are never treated as consent to share a project inquiry.

9. Telephone, email, and text communications

You may request contact regarding a project. Communications may come from Aging at Home Advisor or from independent professionals that received your inquiry. You may revoke consent at any time: text recipients may reply STOP where supported; email recipients may use the unsubscribe mechanism; call recipients may request placement on our internal Do Not Call list. We retain consent records for legal compliance. Consent to marketing is never a condition of purchasing a product or service.

10. Call tracking and recording

Telephone numbers displayed on the website may use call-tracking technology to identify the page, campaign, or source associated with a call. Calls may be monitored or recorded only where permitted by law and after any notice or consent required by applicable law.

Configuration required before launch: CALL_TRACKING_PROVIDER_NAME is not yet selected. Before enabling call tracking, this policy must name the provider, link its privacy policy, and describe the data it processes. Recording remains disabled until state-by-state consent handling, the call announcement, retention, access permissions, and legal review are complete.

11. Data retention

Lead, referral, consent, call-detail, and compliance records

Retained for up to five years after the relevant interaction, or longer when reasonably required by law, litigation hold, dispute, enforcement, or contractual obligation.

Call recordings (if ever enabled)

Retained only for the shortest configured period reasonably necessary for quality, dispute resolution, security, and compliance. The exact period (CALL_RECORDING_RETENTION_PERIOD) will be published once configured — it is intentionally not stated until then.

Analytics and advertising information

Retained according to the configured settings of Google, Meta, and other providers, subject to your choices and applicable law.

Privacy requests and opt-outs

Retained as reasonably necessary to honor the request and demonstrate compliance. Information is deleted, aggregated, or de-identified when no longer reasonably needed.

12. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information — including access limitations, encryption in transit where supported, and vendor diligence. No website or system can guarantee absolute security.
