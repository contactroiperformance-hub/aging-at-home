How We Make Money

Plainly, because you deserve to know: this website is free for consumers. ROI PERFORMANCE LLC may receive compensation when it shares a project request with a home-improvement professional.

Compensation comes from professionals, not from you When you submit a project request and we share it with one or more independent professionals, those businesses may pay us. Compensation may be based on the lead, referral, call, appointment, project, or another commercial arrangement.
Compensation may influence which professionals receive an inquiry Which businesses receive your request can depend on our commercial relationships, along with coverage area and project type. Compensation does not guarantee ranking or endorsement of any professional.
Editorial content is not for sale Guides and comparisons are not altered because a company pays for leads. Cost figures come from published national research, and coverage guidance comes from government sources — see the Editorial Policy.
Sponsored placements are labeled If a placement is ever paid, it will say so visibly. There are currently no sponsored placements on this website.
Not every professional is included We do not claim to show every available professional in your area. A referral is a starting point — comparing multiple written estimates, including from businesses you find yourself, remains the best consumer protection.
No guarantees Compensation arrangements never change this: we do not guarantee contractor performance, pricing, funding, or benefit eligibility, and any work agreement is directly between you and the contractor.

Related: our Editorial Policy explains how content stays independent of these commercial arrangements, and the Privacy Policy explains what happens to your information. Questions: support@agingathomeadvisor.com.

We Value Your Privacy We use analytics and advertising technologies to understand website activity and measure our marketing. You can manage your privacy choices at any time.
